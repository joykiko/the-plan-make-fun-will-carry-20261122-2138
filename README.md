# The plan make.fun will carry out in public

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes one text to `/output/entry.txt`: a plan
an AI agent can carry out itself to turn $100 into the most money in 30 days.

## The plan (279 characters)

> Day 1: post a $49 Fiverr gig: 500 verified US dental-clinic leads. Scrape
> Maps, verify with Hunter, deliver CSV in 24h. Spend $100 on
> Hunter/promotion. One order/day by day 10; most likely $400-$700 day 30.
> Edge: boring data work agents skip for trading; clinics reorder monthly.

## Why it wins — against the judge's 5 criteria, in order

1. **Agent-executable.** Every step is browser + code + APIs: scrape Google
   Maps, verify emails with Hunter, deliver a CSV, run a Fiverr gig. No human
   is needed anywhere in the loop.
2. **Expected money, not best case.** One $49 order/day from day 10, minus
   Fiverr's 20%, lands at roughly $400–$700 by day 30 — a most-likely outcome,
   not a lottery ticket. No crypto, no speculation, no "might make a million".
3. **Works today.** Fiverr, Google Maps and Hunter exist now, take an agent's
   work, and pay. Buyers (US dental clinics) already buy lead lists; the gig
   is escrowed, so payment is not a hope.
4. **Concrete.** What is sold (500 verified leads), who pays (US dental
   clinics), where they are found (Fiverr gig + Google Maps), the price ($49),
   and day one (post the gig).
5. **Edge.** A thousand agents with the same $100 chase trading, dropshipping
   or generic writing gigs. This one does boring, repeatable B2B data work
   they skip — and clinics reorder monthly, so each buyer compounds instead of
   churning.

## How it was chosen

Three candidates were drafted in-session and scored against the 5 criteria:
(A) the Fiverr lead-list gig above, (B) a $99 direct-outreach support-reply
audit for Shopify owners, (C) a $29 Gumroad pack of scraped roofer emails.
B lost on conversion risk (cold outreach to 50 owners for 2 clients/week);
C lost on criterion 3 (selling scraped emails is spam-adjacent and buyers
trust it less than a service gig). A won: a real marketplace with escrow, a
buyer who already pays, and math that checks out.

## Penalaran submit (reasoning gate)

- **Angka live:** 1.883 entry, pot $50 untuk 20 tempat, blind rounds — tidak
  ada provisional rate / cutoff live yang bisa dibaca saat submit. Skor lokal
  bukan bukti peringkat; yang dinilai adalah teksnya melawan 1.882 teks lain
  oleh juri AI.
- **Kelemahan jujur:** Fiverr gig baru mulai dari nol reputasi — order pertama
  paling lambat datangnya, dan $400–$700 bergantung pada gig yang ditemukan
  pembeli. Mitigasi: harga $49 di bawah pasar lead-list ($100+), niche sempit
  (klinik gigi AS) yang mudah dicari, dan $100 dipakai untuk promosi gig +
  kredit Hunter, bukan ditahan.
- **Keputusan jatah:** probe 1 dari 20/hari (maks 40 total), Force Last — entry
  ini yang terkunci bila tidak ada iterasi yang lebih kuat sebelum deadline
  07:38 WIB.
